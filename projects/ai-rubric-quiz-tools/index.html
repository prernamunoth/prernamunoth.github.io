The problem

Building rubrics and quizzes in Canvas by hand is slow. A quiz with 50+ questions took 5 to 6 hours to build, and faculty often had rubrics in Word documents or only assignment descriptions, which then had to be rebuilt in Canvas.

What I built

  • Custom GPTs that faculty used to draft rubrics and quizzes, with my support on the setup and conversion work.
  • A Streamlit app that converts assignment details and existing rubrics or quiz questions into files ready to import into Canvas.
  • A preview step, so a designer or faculty member checks the output before anything goes into a course.
  • Guides for faculty and designers, including how to bring quizzes into New Quizzes through migration.

Results

  • Build time cut by almost 90% across 25+ courses, tracked in a log.
  • 200 rubrics for 50 faculty: 160 created from assignment details and 40 converted from rubrics in documents.
  • 100 quizzes for 25 faculty.
  • First place at the 2026 SMU Technology Showcase.
