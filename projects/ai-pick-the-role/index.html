AI as a Thinking Partner: Pick the Role Before You Prompt

A self-paced Rise course that teaches experienced AI users to choose how AI should help before they prompt: Ask, Coach, Collaborate, Critique or Delegate, based on the task's goals, stakes and how easily the output can be checked.

Problem

Experienced AI users often prompt first and think later. They hand AI work that needs their own judgment, or don't check outputs that matter. They need a quick way to decide how AI should help before they start.

Audience

Professionals who already use AI at work and want to use it more deliberately.

My role

I designed and built the course in Articulate Rise: the objectives, the five-role structure, examples, interactions, knowledge checks and the final quiz.

Design decisions

  • I organized the course around a decision (which role should AI play?) rather than features of AI tools, because that decision is where people go wrong.
  • Every concept uses the same workplace case, a decision about expanding an AI pilot, so learners see one realistic situation from several angles.
  • Learners judge each task on three things: goals, stakes and how easily the output can be checked. High stakes and hard-to-check outputs point away from delegating.
  • Flashcards, tabs and accordions keep the reading short, and knowledge checks after each lesson give practice before the final quiz.
  • An Ask AI Tutor button gives learners help in the moment, practising the skill the course teaches.

Results

Built for a pilot. A six-question quiz with an 80% pass mark measures learning.
